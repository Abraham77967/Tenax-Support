Common questions

Quick answers

Why is an AI import unavailable?

The shared AI route can be temporarily busy or limited by its current quota. Try again later, or choose your own Gemini API key in Tenax Settings if you prefer to use your own quota.

What happens when I sign out?

Your signed-in account data is separated from other accounts. Sign back in to the same account to access its synced data.

How do I delete my account?

Open Tenax Settings, choose the account section, and select Delete Account. This removes the Tenax account data associated with that account and clears local account data on the device.
