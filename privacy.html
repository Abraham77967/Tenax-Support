Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

This policy explains what Tenax collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you use Tenax, you may provide account details through sign-in, including your name, email address, and account identifier. You can also create schedule entries, courses, homework, tasks, captures, notes, and study sessions.

If you use an AI feature, the text, images, PDFs, or other files you choose to submit are sent for processing. If you add your own Gemini API key, Tenax stores it locally in the device’s secure storage so the app can make requests on your behalf.

How Tenax uses information

  • To provide schedules, homework, tasks, captures, reminders, and account sync.
  • To process AI features that you choose to use.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and improve reliability.
  • To respond to support requests.

AI features and Google services

Tenax can use Firebase AI Logic to send your selected AI prompt and attachments to Google Gemini. Firebase AI Logic acts as the connection between the app and Google’s Gemini services. The shared route may record requests and responses in the Tenax Firebase or Google Cloud project for diagnostics and service operation, and it is subject to the applicable Google service terms and policies.

Tenax also supports an optional personal-key route. With that option, your request is sent directly to Google using the Gemini API key you supplied. Tenax does not intentionally upload that personal key to its own database.

Do not submit confidential, financial, medical, or other sensitive information to an AI feature unless you are comfortable with it being processed by the selected AI provider. AI output can be inaccurate and should be checked before you rely on it.

Services used

Tenax uses Firebase services for authentication, database storage, and AI connectivity. Google Gemini may process AI inputs and outputs when you use Tenax AI. These services may receive technical information needed to provide, secure, and operate their services. Their handling of information is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Permissions

Tenax asks for access only when a feature needs it. Depending on what you use, this can include notifications, photos, camera, microphone, or speech recognition. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission may disable the related feature without affecting the rest of the app.

Storage, sign-out, and deletion

Account data is stored in Tenax’s Firebase project and local working data may be cached on your device. Account data is scoped to the signed-in account. Signing out removes that account’s active data from the current session; signing in to another account does not reveal the first account’s data.

You can request deletion from the account section of Tenax Settings. Tenax removes the account data it controls and clears local account data on the device. Some copies may remain temporarily in backups, logs, or third-party systems under their retention rules.

Age

Tenax is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly design Tenax for children under 13.
